## Method

The slip surface is a plane at depth \(z\) parallel to a slope inclined at \(\beta\),
with steady seepage parallel to the slope and the water table at height \(m z\) above the slip plane.

$$
F_s = \frac{c' + (\gamma - m\gamma_w)\, z \cos^2\beta \tan\phi'}{\gamma z \sin\beta \cos\beta}
$$

For a dry cohesionless slope this reduces to \(F_s = \tan\phi' / \tan\beta\).
