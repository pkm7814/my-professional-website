## Namaskar & welcome

I am a geotechnical engineer working on **earthquake geotechnics, seismic slope stability
and disaster-resilient infrastructure**. At IIT Mandi, I am part of the team developing the
**Primer on Disaster Resilient Infrastructure (PoDRI)** in international collaboration with
the University of Canterbury (New Zealand) and Durham University (United Kingdom).

My doctoral research at IIT Roorkee developed analytical methods for the seismic stability of
geosynthetic-reinforced soil slopes, accounting for tension cracks, pore-water pressure and
dynamic loading.
