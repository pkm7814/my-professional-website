  - type: text
    heading: Doctoral research
    body: My Ph.D. at IIT Roorkee, *Dynamic Stability Analysis of Reinforced Soil Slope Considering Tension Cracks*, developed limit-equilibrium-based pseudo-static and pseudo-dynamic formulations for the **internal and external stability of geosynthetic-reinforced soil slopes**. The work quantifies how tension cracks, water forces in the cracks and pore-water pressure reduce stability and increase the reinforcement required under seismic loading.
