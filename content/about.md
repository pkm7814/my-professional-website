      I am a geotechnical earthquake engineer and Senior Project Scientist (Postdoctoral Researcher) at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Mandi, working on multi-hazard resilient infrastructure. My research bridges advanced theoretical mechanics and global policy frameworks for disaster risk reduction.

      My Ph.D. at IIT Roorkee developed seismic stability analyses for geosynthetic-reinforced soil slopes under the combined effects of tension cracks and pore-water pressure. I now apply this expertise within an international academic consortium, with the University of Canterbury (New Zealand) and Durham University (United Kingdom), to develop the **Primer on Disaster Resilient Infrastructure (PoDRI)**, which aims to support consistent engineering practice for disaster risk reduction worldwide.
