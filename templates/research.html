Research & Expertise

Core Research Areas

  • Advanced Geotechnical Modeling: Nonlinear soil behavior, modified pseudo-dynamic approaches, and analysis using numerical software (PLAXIS, MIDAS GTS NX, OpenSees).

Current Focus

My work at IIT Mandi involves developing robust engineering frameworks and methodologies to mitigate geotechnical hazards in mountainous and vulnerable regions.
