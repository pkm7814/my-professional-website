About Me

Senior Project Scientist (Postdoctoral Researcher)
School of Civil and Environmental Engineering, IIT Mandi

Research Profile

I am currently serving as a Senior Project Scientist (Postdoctoral Researcher) at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Mandi, specializing in earthquake geotechnics, soil dynamics, and disaster-resilient infrastructure. In this role, I work as part of a collaborative team on the development of the Primer on Disaster Resilient Infrastructure (PODRI) in close international partnership.

My collaborative and supervisory team includes:

  • Dr. Ashutosh Kumar (Associate Professor), School of Civil and Environmental Engineering, IIT Mandi, India
  • Dr. Ellen Robson, University of Canterbury, New Zealand
  • Prof. Bruce Malamud, Institute of Hazard, Risk and Resilience, Durham University, United Kingdom
  • Dr. Nicola Thounaojam, IIT Mandi, India
  • Abigail Underwood, University of Canterbury, New Zealand

My foundational research expertise is backed by my Ph.D. in Geotechnical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT), where my doctoral dissertation focused on the advanced seismic stability analysis of geosynthetic-reinforced soil slopes, examining the complex impacts of tension cracks, pore water pressures, and dynamic loading conditions.
