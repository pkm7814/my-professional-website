About Me

Research Profile

Senior Project Scientist (Postdoctoral) at IIT Mandi specializing in earthquake geotechnics and disaster-resilient infrastructure. Currently leading the development of the Primer on Disaster Resilient Infrastructure (PODRI) in international collaboration with the University of Canterbury (New Zealand) and Durham University (UK). Backed by a Ph.D. from IIT Roorkee focused on the seismic stability analysis of geosynthetic-reinforced soil slopes.
